Regulatory Tool · Free Utility

Timber Import Scope Checker

Indicative scoping guidance for timber and timber-product imports. Enter the country of harvest, HS code and (optionally) species — the tool returns scope assessments against EUDR, UKTR, EUTR transitional, US Lacey Act and the Australian ILP Act, and flags any CITES-listed species.

EUDR 2023/1115 — EU UKTR 2013 — GB EUTR 995/2010 — EU (transitional) Lacey Act 2008 — US ILP Act 2012 — AU CITES — Global
⚠ ADVISORY USE ONLY — This tool provides indicative scoping guidance based on published regulatory frameworks. It does not constitute legal advice. HS code classification, species identification, and country of harvest determination must be confirmed by a qualified customs agent or legal adviser. Regulatory scope determinations may change with updates to legislation.
Private by design. This tool runs in your browser. No data is sent to a server, stored, or shared. Use it as often as you need.
