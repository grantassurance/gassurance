About these tools

Built from regulatory practice — not marketing copy

These tools are built from current regulatory texts and the practical experience of advising operators, traders and certification bodies. Each one is designed to answer a specific question quickly — without account creation, signup or data capture.

The tools provide indicative guidance only and do not replace legal advice or formal due diligence. Where a definitive determination is needed, get in touch.

No signup. No data capture. No tracking.

Our tools run entirely in your browser. We do not ask for your name, email, company or any other personal information. Nothing you enter is stored, transmitted to a server, or used for marketing. You can use these tools as often as you like, without creating an account.

Timber Import Scope Checker

Check whether timber and timber-product imports fall within EUDR, UKTR, EUTR transitional, US Lacey Act and Australian ILP Act scope. Enter HS code, country of harvest and species — returns scope assessments and CITES alerts in seconds.
